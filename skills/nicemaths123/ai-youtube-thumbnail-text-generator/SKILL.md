## 🔤 Font Recommendations

For maximum readability:
- **Primary font:** Bold, sans-serif (Impact, Montserrat, Roboto)
- **Secondary font:** Clean, readable (Open Sans, Lato)
- **Font size:** 40-60px for main text, 20-30px for secondary
- **Contrast:** Always use high contrast (light text on dark background or vice versa)

---

## 📏 Size Guidelines

- **Recommended:** 1280x720 pixels (16:9)
- **Minimum text size:** 40px for main text
- **Safe zone:** Keep text 20% away from edges
- **File size:** Under 2MB for fast loading
