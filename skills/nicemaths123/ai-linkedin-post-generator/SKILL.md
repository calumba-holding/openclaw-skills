## 🎯 Viral LinkedIn Post Structures

### Structure 1: The "Story + Lesson" Hook
```
I made a mistake that cost me [amount/time]
Here's what I learned:
[3 lessons]
Lesson 1: [specific insight]
Lesson 2: [specific insight]
Lesson 3: [specific insight]
If I could go back, I'd [action]
What would you do differently?
```

### Structure 2: The "Contrarian Take" Hook
```
Unpopular opinion: [bold statement]
Here's why I believe this:
[3 reasons]
1. [reason 1]
2. [reason 2]
3. [reason 3]
Most people disagree with me.
But here's what I've seen:
[personal experience]
Agree or disagree? Let's discuss.
```

### Structure 3: The "Listicle" Hook
```
7 things I learned from [experience]:
1. [insight 1]
2. [insight 2]
3. [insight 3]
4. [insight 4]
5. [insight 5]
6. [insight 6]
7. [insight 7]
Which one resonates with you most?
```

### Structure 4: The "Data-Backed" Hook
```
I analyzed 100 [companies/campaigns/strategies]
Here's what I found:
[3 surprising insights]
Insight 1: [data-backed finding]
Insight 2: [data-backed finding]
Insight 3: [data-backed finding]
The data doesn't lie.
What's your experience with this?
```

### Structure 5: The "Transformation" Hook
```
3 years ago: [before state]
Today: [after state]
Here's exactly how I did it:
Step 1: [action]
Step 2: [action]
Step 3: [action]
The key was [key insight]
What transformation are you working toward?
```

## 📊 Engagement Scoring System

Each post gets an **Engagement Score** based on:
- Hook strength (0-30 points)
- Value provided (0-25 points)
- CTA effectiveness (0-25 points)
- Format optimization (0-20 points)

**Score breakdown:**
- 80-100: Viral potential (1000+ likes)
- 60-79: High engagement (500-1000 likes)
- 40-59: Moderate engagement (100-500 likes)
- 20-39: Low engagement (10-100 likes)
- 0-19: Poor performance (<10 likes)

## 📝 Post Formats

### Text-Only
- Best for: Personal stories, insights, opinions
- Length: 1,300-3,000 characters
- Engagement: High when authentic

### Image + Text
- Best for: Data visualizations, quotes, before/after
- Image size: 1200x627 pixels
- Engagement: 2x text-only

### Video + Text
- Best for: Tutorials, presentations, demos
- Length: 30-90 seconds
- Engagement: 3x text-only

### Document + Text
- Best for: Guides, templates, checklists
- Format: PDF carousel
- Engagement: High lead generation
