## 🎯 Press Release Structures

### Structure 1: The Launch Announcement
```
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
[Company Name] Launches [Product/Service], Revolutionizing [Industry]

[CITY, Date] — [Company Name], a leader in [industry], today announced the launch of [product/service], a [key benefit] solution that [problem solved].

[Company spokesperson quote]

Key features of [product/service] include:
• [Feature 1]: [Benefit]
• [Feature 2]: [Benefit]
• [Feature 3]: [Benefit]

[Additional context or statistics]

About [Company Name]: [Brief company description]

Media Contact: [Name, Email, Phone]
[Website URL]
```

## 📊 Media Pickup Scoring System

Each release gets a **Media Pickup Score** based on:
- Newsworthiness (0-30 points)
- Headline quality (0-25 points)
- Quote authenticity (0-25 points)
- Distribution readiness (0-20 points)

**Score breakdown:**
- 80-100: High pickup potential (50+ outlets)
- 60-79: Good pickup potential (20-50 outlets)
- 40-59: Moderate pickup (5-20 outlets)
- 20-39: Low pickup (1-5 outlets)
- 0-19: Poor pickup (0 outlets)

## 📰 Headline Optimization

Journalist-optimized headlines:
- **News-focused:** "Company X Launches Y, Transforming Z Industry"
- **Data-driven:** "New Study Reveals 73% of Businesses Struggle With X"
- **Trend-aligned:** "Company X Announces First Y in Z Market"
- **Problem-solution:** "Company X Solves [Problem] With New [Solution]"
- **Milestone-focused:** "Company X Reaches [Milestone], First in Industry"

---

## 💬 Quote Generation

Professional quotes that sound authentic:
- **CEO quotes:** Vision, impact, future outlook
- **Product quotes:** Features, benefits, customer value
- **Customer quotes:** Results, experience, transformation
- **Expert quotes:** Industry insight, validation, context

## 💡 Pro Tips

- Lead with the news, not the company
- Include a compelling human quote
- Keep it under 500 words
- Provide multimedia (images, video)
- Follow up with journalists personally
- Time releases for Tuesday-Thursday mornings
- Make it easy for journalists: include all contact info
